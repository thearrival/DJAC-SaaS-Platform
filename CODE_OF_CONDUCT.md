# Code of Conduct — DJAC Platform Community

## Our Commitment

The DJAC platform community is committed to providing a welcoming, inclusive, and harassment-free experience for all participants, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Code of Conduct Standards

### Expected Behavior

All community members are expected to:

- **Be respectful and inclusive** — Use welcoming and respectful language in all interactions
- **Be collaborative** — Work constructively toward shared goals and community benefit
- **Be transparent** — Communicate openly and honestly about intentions and limitations
- **Be professional** — Conduct yourself in a manner that reflects well on the DJAC platform and its community
- **Respect privacy** — Do not share others' personal information without explicit permission
- **Value diversity** — Recognize that diverse perspectives strengthen the platform

### Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated within the DJAC community:

- **Harassment or bullying** — Sustained disruption, offensive comments, or intimidation
- **Discrimination** — Any form of exclusion or prejudice based on protected characteristics
- **Sexual harassment** — Unwelcome sexual advances, comments, or requests for sexual favors
- **Threats or violence** — Threats of harm, encouragement of self-harm, or glorification of violence
- **Hate speech** — Content that promotes discrimination, intolerance, or hatred against individuals or groups
- **Trolling and disruption** — Deliberate off-topic posting, inflammatory comments, or interference with community operations
- **Privacy violations** — Publishing others' private information without consent (doxxing)
- **Unprofessional conduct** — Behavior that disrupts the community's ability to function productively

## Reporting Violations

### How to Report

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it to the maintainers through one of the following channels:

1. **Email** — Send a detailed report to the maintainers at the contact address listed in the project's SECURITY.md or README
2. **Private Issue** — Open a private issue on GitHub (do not use public issues for sensitive reports)
3. **Direct Contact** — Reach out to a project maintainer directly

### What to Include

When reporting a violation, please provide as much detail as possible:

- **Your identity** (optional — anonymous reports are accepted)
- **Date and time** of the incident
- **Location** (platform, forum, channel, or event)
- **Description** of the behavior that violated this code
- **Impact** on you or the community
- **Supporting evidence** (screenshots, logs, messages) — if available and you are comfortable sharing

### Response Commitment

- **Acknowledgment**: Reports will be acknowledged within **48 hours**
- **Investigation**: A thorough investigation will be conducted within **5 business days**
- **Action**: Appropriate corrective action will be taken based on the findings
- **Confidentiality**: All reports and investigations will be handled with discretion

## Enforcement Policy

### Enforcement Levels

The DJAC maintainers reserve the right to take corrective action proportionate to the severity of the violation:

| Level        | Behavior                                                       | Action                                                           |
| ------------ | -------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Minor**    | Inadvertent offensive language, mild disagreements             | Private warning and educational conversation                     |
| **Moderate** | Repeated offenses, persistent disruption, inflammatory conduct | Temporary suspension from community channels (1–30 days)         |
| **Severe**   | Harassment, discrimination, threats, privacy violations        | Permanent ban from the DJAC platform and all associated channels |
| **Critical** | Illegal activity, credible threats of violence                 | Immediate ban and referral to law enforcement                    |

### Investigation Process

1. **Triage**: Maintainers review the report and determine its validity and severity
2. **Gather Facts**: Maintainers may contact the reporter, the accused party, and any witnesses
3. **Assessment**: The maintainers evaluate the evidence and determine the appropriate response
4. **Resolution**: Corrective action is implemented and communicated to the involved parties
5. **Follow-up**: The maintainers monitor the situation to ensure the issue is fully resolved

### Appeals

If a party disagrees with the enforcement decision, they may appeal by:

1. Sending a written appeal to the project maintainers within **7 business days**
2. The maintainers will review the appeal and provide a response within **10 business days**
3. The appeal decision is final and binding

### Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to:

- All community spaces (GitHub repositories, Discord, forums, and chat channels)
- All public events, conferences, and meetups related to DJAC
- All private communications in a professional context related to DJAC
- All online and offline interactions when representing the DJAC project

### Maintainer Responsibilities

Maintainers are expected to:

- Enforce this Code of Conduct consistently and fairly
- Treat all reports with confidentiality and sensitivity
- Avoid conflicts of interest in investigations
- Lead by example through their own conduct
- Provide transparent and timely communication

## Contact

For questions, concerns, or to report a violation, please contact the DJAC maintainers through the channels listed in the project documentation.
