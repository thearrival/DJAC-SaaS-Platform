# 05 Industry Editions

## Edition Strategy

Industry editions should be configuration-driven overlays, not separate codebases. An edition selects the most relevant framework packs, dashboards, AI agents, reports, and control views for a sector.

## Target Editions

- DJAC Finance
- DJAC Healthcare
- DJAC Government
- DJAC AI
- DJAC Cloud
- DJAC Telecom
- DJAC Energy
- DJAC Manufacturing
- DJAC Retail
- DJAC Education
- DJAC Critical Infrastructure
- DJAC Defense
- DJAC Smart Cities

## Edition Behavior

Each edition should:

- auto-select relevant regulatory frameworks
- surface sector-specific risk indicators
- prioritize appropriate AI agents and playbooks
- preconfigure board and executive reports
- highlight sector-specific evidence and control libraries

## Implementation Principle

The same underlying product should power every edition. Only the defaults, templates, and recommendation weights should vary by sector.
