# 01 Vision

## Objective

Evolve DJAC into the AI operating system for global compliance intelligence while preserving the current product identity, workflows, APIs, dashboards, and architecture.

## Product Principles

- Preserve the DJAC brand, navigation style, and existing user experience.
- Extend the current platform instead of replacing it.
- Keep all existing compliance flows functional and backward-compatible.
- Treat regulations as structured intelligence, not static reference content.
- Make every new capability modular, event-driven, observable, and API-first.
- Support international expansion without duplicating code paths per region.

## What DJAC Becomes

DJAC becomes a unified intelligence layer for:

- Cross-border compliance
- Regulatory interpretation and comparison
- AI governance and model risk
- Cybersecurity control mapping
- Vendor and third-party risk
- Continuous compliance monitoring
- Evidence automation and audit readiness
- Board and executive reporting

## Non-Goals

- Replacing the current app with a new product line.
- Breaking existing framework comparisons, assessments, or reports.
- Introducing a parallel data model for the same concepts.
- Creating edition-specific forks of the codebase.

## Success Criteria

- The platform can support new jurisdictions without changing the core product shape.
- Existing Saudi Arabia and China functionality continues to work unchanged.
- Users can search, compare, translate, and operationalize regulations across regions.
- AI responses are grounded in a shared compliance knowledge graph.
